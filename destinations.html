When is the Best Time to Visit?

Sri Lanka has two distinct monsoon seasons affecting opposite coasts, meaning there is always a sunny region year-round!

South & West Coasts

Galle, Mirissa, Bentota, Colombo

Best from December to April with dry, calm seas and prime whale-watching conditions.

Peak Season: Dec – Mar

Cultural Triangle & East

Sigiriya, Anuradhapura, Trincomalee, Pasikuda

Best from May to September. Ideal for diving, beaches, and ancient ruins explorations.

Great in European Summer

Central Highlands

Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Horton Plains

Pleasantly cool year-round (15–22°C). Best hiking and tea trail walks from January to April.

Year-Round Spring Climate
